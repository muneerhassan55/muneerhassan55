I build mobile apps that handle money, AI and real-time features in production. Mostly Flutter, with NestJS or Laravel backends when the product needs one. I care about the parts users never see: webhooks that don't double-charge, keys that never ship in the app binary, and releases that pass store review the first time.

## 🧭 What I work on

**💳 Payments**
Stripe, Paymob, Moyasar, Fatora, RevenueCat and native in-app purchases, plus Coinbase and Yellow Card for crypto on/off-ramp. Server-side webhooks, idempotency, refunds and daily reconciliation, not just the checkout screen.

**🤖 AI features**
GPT-4o vision that writes a product listing from one photo in English, Urdu and Hindi. Picture-to-speech for children with speech difficulties. Natural-language goals turned into structured plans.

**📡 Streaming & real-time**
IPTV players (Xtream/M3U, EPG, HLS), live streaming and group voice rooms with ZEGOCLOUD and Agora, a WireGuard VPN app with subscriptions.

**🛠️ Backend & release**
NestJS + PostgreSQL, Laravel, Firebase. CI/CD with GitHub Actions, crash monitoring, iOS privacy manifests and Play Store data-safety compliance.
